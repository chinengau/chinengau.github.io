The Loser Janitor is the Ultimate Legend

Synopsis劇情簡介

Twenty years ago, Grand Lord Cyril ended an alien invasion but lost his wife. He vanished into a janitor’s life, raising his son and refusing wealth or titles. After saving a girl, he’s set up on a date with her mother—a tycoon. Mocked as unworthy, he stays silent until he reveals his identity, humbling his tormentors. Then the aliens return.

二十年前，大領主 Cyril 終結了一場外星入侵，卻失去了妻子。他從此隱姓埋名成為清潔工，獨自扶養兒子，拒絕一切財富與頭銜。一次救了小女孩後，他被安排與女孩的母親——一位企業女強人——相親。面對眾人嘲諷，他始終沉默，直到亮出真實身分讓所有人低頭。而此時，外星人再度來襲。
